# Essays behind the book

These essays were first written on the essay surface, where notes don't last, and were moved here on 2026-11-15 so the parked book keeps them. They're working drafts, not chapters.

- `spike-five-chapters.md`: the five-chapter spike, organized by kinds of work with the cadence as its spine. Pick the book up here.
- `the-menu.md`: cookbooks, recipes and the missing word: if the agents do the cooking, what does the book's reader need?
- `the-food-lab.md`: The Food Lab as the book's cookbook touchstone, beside Moby Dick and K&R.
- `shale-on-the-landing-page.md`: the landing-page morning, which showed the four beats of the cadence in one hour.
- `an-early-morning.md`: a look back at one morning: a cookbook for the book, a spike, a landing page shipped, and the warts it showed in Shale.
- `ten-theses.md`: ten ways to say what the book is about; numbers 4, 7 and 9 are curveballs.
