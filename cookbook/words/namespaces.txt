-- Namespace prefixes: a device's or a firmware's name that starts an
-- effect's name (`block_read_sector!`, `uefi_read_key!`, `port_out_32!`),
-- with its act after it. The Roc name hunt's `noun_effect` and
-- `state_effect` judge the word after the prefix, as they judge an
-- effect's first word: the prefix is a namespace, and the act follows it.
-- A name that is the prefix alone (`port!`) is judged as it stands.
--
-- One prefix a line, then the namespace's own acts, if any: words that
-- are no verb in English but are this device's instructions, so that
-- `port_out_32!` (x86's OUT) is an act and `port_status!` still a noun.
