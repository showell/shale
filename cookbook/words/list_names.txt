-- Words the Roc name hunt's `singular_list` can't read from their spelling.
-- A word per line, then its kind:
--   many   a word that can't be counted, so it has no plural;
--   one    a word that names one thing though it reads otherwise: an `s` that
--          makes no plural, or a verb of verbs.txt that is a noun in a list's
--          name;
--   after  a word that follows the noun, so a plural before it counts
--          (`corners_ahead`);
--   accumulator  a short idiom for a list being built, as a whole name only.
-- A collective noun is no `many`: a herd holds cows, so `herd_cows`. Only a word
-- a tree needs, or that Steve named, belongs here. `--` starts a note.
grass many -- Steve's example of a word with no plural; no tree uses it yet
axis one -- Steve's example
canvas one -- Steve's example
status one -- `status : List(Str)` (widget_kit's VcrButtons.roc)
us one -- `toward_us` (safari's BirdStills.roc)
route one -- verbs.txt has it for effects; a route of segments is `route_segments`
ahead after -- `corners_ahead` (safari's Ground.roc)
along after -- `quads_along` (safari's GuardRail.roc)
down after -- `side_wings_down` (safari's BirdStills.roc)
holding after -- `humans_holding` (roc-chat's GitHubSignIn.roc)
up after -- `side_wings_up` (safari's BirdStills.roc)
out accumulator -- Steve: "accumulators can have short idioms"
