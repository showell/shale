# cookbook/roc/EXAMPLES.txt: the Roc-on-Shale examples, one a line, for the
# examples page, in sections. A section starts with its line: `## `, its
# heading, a tab, and one sentence saying what is in it; the examples under
# it, to the next section line, are its own, in the page's order. An example
# line has five fields, tab-separated: the name; the Roc's path (an app's
# root, its modules beside it); the Shale original's path; the commit that
# replaced the original with the Roc, so `git show <commit>~1:<original>` is
# the original as it last stood; and one sentence of what it does. A new
# port goes at the end of its section, not of the file. A commit is this
# repository's, and moves if its branch is rebased. Any other line that
# starts with `#` is a comment, as these are.
